Sky modifications: selected 32 objects; converted sexagesimal J2000
coordinates to decimal hours/degrees; added Thai display names and
educational descriptions, categories, search aliases and image framing;
preserved source identifiers, field source codes and missing magnitudes;
withheld two magnitude values pending verification.

The adapted catalog in src/lib/deepSky.js and its accompanying catalog
provenance/educational descriptions are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.
This does not change licenses applicable to other application code.

Data sources acknowledged by OpenNGC: NASA/IPAC Extragalactic Database
(NED), HyperLEDA, SIMBAD operated at CDS Strasbourg, HEASARC, and
Harold Corwin's NGC/IC Positions and Notes. See the original README
and the Sources field for attribution of individual fields.

See public/data/deep-sky-sources.md for source URLs, checksums,
modifications, uncertainty, coordinate limitations and image attribution.
